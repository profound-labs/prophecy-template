# Glossary

<a id="top" class="top-anchor">top</a>

**anicca:**
  (Pāli) Impermanence: one of the *three characteristics of existence*
  along with not-self (*anattā*) and unsatisfactoriness (*dukkha*).
  [← top](#top)

**borapet:**
  (Thai) Tinospora crispa. Heart-shaped moonseed or guduchi. An
  extremely bitter vine used as a prophylactic and treatment for
  malaria.
  [← top](#top)
